# Claude Certification Study Notes

A free, complete study guide for Anthropic's four Claude certification exams: Associate, Foundations; Developer, Foundations; Architect, Foundations; and Architect, Professional. For every domain of every exam you get what it measures, how the questions look, a step-by-step method, the traps that cost points, timing advice and 3 to 5 original practice items with full explanations. Updated for the **December 7, 2026** exam changes. Every exam fact comes from Anthropic's official exam guides, its certification FAQ or Pearson VUE, each one [cited](docs/sources.md).

Want to see where you stand first? Each exam has a free timed diagnostic: [Associate](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/claude-certified-associate?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=p5gza7&utm_content=claude-certified-associate), [Developer](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/claude-certified-developer?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=p5gza7&utm_content=claude-certified-developer), [Architect Foundations](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/claude-certified-architect?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=p5gza7&utm_content=claude-certified-architect), [Architect Professional](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/claude-certified-architect-professional?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=p5gza7&utm_content=claude-certified-architect-professional).

## Start here

1. **Pick your exam and version.** Read [which exam, and which version](docs/choosing-an-exam.md). If you test on or after December 7, 2026, three of the four exams change.
2. **Find your weak domains.** Do the [diagnostic](docs/diagnostic.md): Item 1 on every domain page, timed, scored by domain.
3. **Study the weakest heavy domain first,** then follow the [7-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#7-day-plan), or the [2-day plan](docs/study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if your exam is close.

## Quick facts

| | Associate, Foundations | Developer, Foundations | Architect, Foundations | Architect, Professional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Code | CCAO-F | CCDV-F | CCAR-F | CCAR-P |
| Items from December 7, 2026 | 70 (60 scored) | 70 (60 scored) | 60 (no update) | 70 (60 scored) |
| Items through December 6, 2026 | 60 | 53 | 60 | 63 |
| Time | 120 min | 120 min | 120 min | 120 min |
| Pass mark | 720 of 1,000 (scaled) | 720 | 720 | 720 |
| Domains | 6 | 8 | 5 | 7 |
| Heaviest domain | Output Evaluation and Quality Control, 20% | Applications and Integration, 20% | Agentic Architecture and Orchestration, 27% | Integration, 21% |
| Notes | [Open](docs/associate/README.md) | [Open](docs/developer/README.md) | [Open](docs/architect-foundations/README.md) | [Open](docs/architect-professional/README.md) |

All four: closed book, delivered by Pearson VUE online or at a test center, valid for 12 months, open to people at Claude Partner Network organizations. Sources: the official exam guides and the [Partner Academy certifications page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications), checked 24 November 2026.

## How to use this repo

- **Read on GitHub.** Every page links to the previous page, the next page and back to these contents.
- **Go by weight.** Each exam's overview page lists its domains with weights and approximate item counts. Spend your time where the points are.
- **Test yourself honestly.** Answers are hidden under "Answer and explanation". Commit to an answer first.
- **Read every explanation,** including for items you got right. Each one says why every wrong option is wrong, which is where most of the learning is.
- **Do the work, not just the reading.** These exams test judgment in real scenarios. The study plans pair every domain with a hands-on task.
- **Use it as a GitBook space** if you prefer: the structure is in [SUMMARY.md](SUMMARY.md).

## About the practice items

All practice items are original, written for these notes. None are taken from Anthropic's exams, its exam guides or any prep site, and none are recalled exam questions. They are written against the published domains and skill statements so that the judgment you build here transfers to the real exam.

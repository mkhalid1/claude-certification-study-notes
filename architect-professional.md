# Claude Certified Architect, Professional (CCAR-P)

63 items, 120 minutes, scaled pass mark of 720 on a 100 to 1,000 scale. For senior architects who design and govern Claude at enterprise scale. No formal prerequisite.

## Domains and weights

| # | Domain | Weight | About this many of 63 items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Solution Design and Architecture | 17% | 11 |
| 2 | Claude Models, Prompting and Context Engineering | 13% | 8 |
| 3 | Integration | 19% | 12 |
| 4 | Evaluation, Testing and Optimization | 16% | 10 |
| 5 | Governance, Safety and Risk Management | 14% | 9 |
| 6 | Stakeholder Communication and Lifecycle Management | 14% | 9 |
| 7 | Developer Productivity and Operational Enablement | 7% | 4 |

Integration is the largest domain. Each item usually turns on one deciding constraint, such as data residency, an evaluation gap or a stakeholder requirement, so read the stem for that constraint before the options.

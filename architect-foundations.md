# Claude Certified Architect, Foundations (CCAR-F)

60 items, 120 minutes, scaled pass mark of 720 on a 100 to 1,000 scale. Questions are built on official scenarios such as a customer support agent, a multi-agent research system, Claude Code in CI and structured data extraction.

## Domains and weights

| # | Domain | Weight | About this many of 60 items |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Agentic Architecture and Orchestration | 27% | 16 |
| 2 | Tool Design and MCP Integration | 18% | 11 |
| 3 | Claude Code Configuration and Workflows | 20% | 12 |
| 4 | Prompt Engineering and Structured Output | 20% | 12 |
| 5 | Context Management and Reliability | 15% | 9 |

Agent loops and orchestration are over a quarter of the exam. Expect questions on when to use subagents, how to pass context between them, when to escalate to a person, and how to keep tool calls reliable.

## About exam dumps

Dumps are recalled real items, often wrong and against the exam rules. Scenario-based questions also change form to form, so memorized answers do not transfer. Practice on original questions in the same format instead.

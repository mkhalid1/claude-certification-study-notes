# Which exam, and which version

Anthropic runs four Claude certification exams across three roles: Associate, Developer and Architect. This page helps you pick the right one and explains the December 7, 2026 update, which changes three of the four exams.

## The four exams side by side

| | Associate, Foundations | Developer, Foundations | Architect, Foundations | Architect, Professional |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Code | CCAO-F | CCDV-F | CCAR-F | CCAR-P |
| Built for | Consultants, sellers and delivery leads who guide customers to the right Claude use cases | Engineers who build with the Claude API, Claude Code and MCP | Practitioners who make trade-off decisions when building production solutions with Claude Code, the Agent SDK, the Claude API and MCP | Senior architects who design and govern Claude at enterprise scale |
| Items (from December 7, 2026) | 70, of which 60 scored | 70, of which 60 scored | 60 (not part of the update) | 70, of which 60 scored |
| Time | 120 minutes | 120 minutes | 120 minutes | 120 minutes |
| Pass mark | 720 on a 100 to 1,000 scale | 720 | 720 | 720 |
| Domains | 6 | 8 | 5 | 7 |
| Valid for | 12 months | 12 months | 12 months | 12 months |
| Study notes | [Associate](associate/README.md) | [Developer](developer/README.md) | [Architect Foundations](architect-foundations/README.md) | [Architect Professional](architect-professional/README.md) |

All four are closed book and delivered by Pearson VUE, either online with a proctor (OnVUE) or at a test center. Sources: the four official exam guides and the [Partner Academy certifications page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications), checked 24 November 2026.

## Which one should you take?

- **You advise customers, sell Claude, or run delivery, and do not write code:** start with [Associate, Foundations](associate/README.md). It tests judgment in everyday work: framing tasks, checking output, choosing features and models, and handling data responsibly.
- **You write code against the Claude API, Claude Code or MCP:** take [Developer, Foundations](developer/README.md). It is the most hands-on exam: agents, integration, error handling, evals, model choice and tools.
- **You design the system and own the trade-offs:** take [Architect, Foundations](architect-foundations/README.md). Its questions sit inside production scenarios such as a support agent, a multi-agent research system or Claude Code in CI.
- **You already lead enterprise Claude deployments:** consider [Architect, Professional](architect-professional/README.md). Anthropic's FAQ says Foundations is the natural place to start, but there is no formal prerequisite, and Foundations does not upgrade to Professional automatically.

If two fit, pick the one closest to the work you do every week. These exams reward experience more than reading.

## The December 7, 2026 update

Anthropic's certifications page says: "Three exams update on December 7, 2026. Use the guide that matches your exam date." Pearson VUE's [Anthropic page](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html) says the same for Associate, Foundations; Developer, Foundations; and Architect, Professional.

**The date you sit the exam decides the version.** If you test on December 6 or earlier, you take the current exam. If you test on December 7 or later, you take the updated one.

| | Through December 6, 2026 | December 7, 2026 or later |
|---|---|---|
| Associate, Foundations | 60 items, 7 domains | 70 items (60 scored, 10 unscored), 6 domains |
| Developer, Foundations | 53 items, 8 domains | 70 items (60 scored, 10 unscored), 8 domains with new weights |
| Architect, Professional | 63 items, 7 domains | 70 items (60 scored, 10 unscored), 7 domains with new weights |
| Architect, Foundations | 60 items, 5 domains | No change |

What stays the same on the updated exams: 120 minutes, a scaled score from 100 to 1,000 and a pass mark of 720.

**These notes follow the December 7 version** for the three updated exams, because most people studying now will sit the exam after that date. Each exam's overview page has a side-by-side table of the old and new domains and weights, so you can adjust if you test earlier.

## Who can sit the exams

Certification is currently open only to people at organizations in the Claude Partner Network. Anthropic's [eligibility page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/certification-eligibility) adds:

- you sign in with a company email on a domain listed in your organization's partner record (a personal address will not work)
- you need to be 18 or older and have a government-issued ID
- if your organization is not a partner, you cannot register, but the public Claude Academy courses end with a quiz and a certificate of completion

A certificate of completion from a course is not the same as a certification. Only the proctored Pearson exam gives you the credential and the Credly badge.

# Exam-day checklist

All four exams are delivered by Pearson VUE, online with a proctor (OnVUE) or at a test center. Facts here come from Anthropic's [certification FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications) and [Pearson VUE's Anthropic page](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/anthropic.html), checked 24 November 2026.

## A week before

- [ ] Check your exam date against December 7, 2026. That date decides which exam version and guide apply. See [which version you will sit](choosing-an-exam.md#the-december-7-2026-update).
- [ ] Make sure the name in your exam profile matches your government-issued ID exactly.
- [ ] If testing online on a work laptop or corporate network, ask IT to check Pearson's network requirements. Pearson's Anthropic page lists the domains that must be reachable.
- [ ] If English is your second language, the FAQ explains how to request a time extension through Pearson. It takes 3 to 5 business days to process, so do it early.
- [ ] Know the deadlines: test center appointments can be rescheduled or cancelled up to 48 hours before, and OnVUE appointments up to one hour before. Missing the window or not showing up forfeits the fee.

## The day before

- [ ] Run Pearson's system test on the exact computer and network you will use.
- [ ] Find your confirmation email with the "Check in to start exam" link (OnVUE).
- [ ] Plan for about 135 minutes of seat time: 120 minutes of exam plus check-in, instructions and a short survey.
- [ ] Light review only: the traps lists and the "Key ideas to know cold" tables.

## Before you start

- [ ] Government-issued ID ready.
- [ ] For OnVUE: a quiet, private room with a clear desk. Close every other app.
- [ ] Water and a bathroom break before check-in.

## During the exam

- [ ] The exam is closed book. No notes, documentation, translation tools or AI assistants.
- [ ] Each item tells you how many responses to select. Read that line first.
- [ ] Find the deciding constraint in the stem before reading the options.
- [ ] Pace: about 1.7 minutes per item on the 70-item exams, 2 minutes on Architect, Foundations. Check the clock every 15 items or so.
- [ ] Do not try to spot unscored items. Treat every item as if it counts.
- [ ] Never leave an item blank.

## After the exam

- [ ] Your score appears on screen. Note your weakest domains while they are fresh.
- [ ] If you passed, accept your badge from the Credly email.
- [ ] If you did not pass, the earliest retake is 14 days after a first attempt. Use the domain breakdown to plan. See [scoring and retakes](scoring.md#retakes).

## Last practice before the real one

If you have a day, one more timed run helps more than more reading. The free diagnostics for each exam are listed on the [diagnostic page](diagnostic.md#option-2-a-free-timed-diagnostic).

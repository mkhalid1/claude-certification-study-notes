# Frequently asked questions

The questions people search for most about the Claude certifications, drawn from Google search results in November 2026 and from the questions Anthropic answers in its own [certification FAQ](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/faq-certifications). Answers stick to what Anthropic and Pearson VUE state, and say so when the answer is our guidance.

## What Claude certifications are there?

Four: Claude Certified Associate, Foundations (CCAO-F); Claude Certified Developer, Foundations (CCDV-F); Claude Certified Architect, Foundations (CCAR-F); and Claude Certified Architect, Professional (CCAR-P). See [which exam to take](choosing-an-exam.md).

## Who can take the exams?

People at organizations in the Claude Partner Network, signing in with a company email on a domain listed in the organization's partner record. You must be 18 or older and have a government-issued ID. Anthropic says certification is not currently available to customers or non-partners.

## How many questions are on the exam, and how long is it?

From December 7, 2026: 70 items (60 scored, 10 unscored) in 120 minutes for Associate, Foundations; Developer, Foundations; and Architect, Professional. Architect, Foundations has 60 items in 120 minutes. Plan for about 135 minutes of total seat time.

## What is the passing score?

720 on a scaled range of 100 to 1,000, for all four exams. See [how scoring works](scoring.md).

## What changes on December 7, 2026?

Associate, Foundations; Developer, Foundations; and Architect, Professional get updated content, new domain weights and a 70-item format. The date you test decides which version you take. Architect, Foundations is not part of the update. See [the December 7 update](choosing-an-exam.md#the-december-7-2026-update).

## Is there a Claude certification exam guide PDF?

Yes. Each exam has an official guide, and the three updating exams have two: one for exams through December 6 and one for December 7 onward. They are linked from the [Partner Academy certifications page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications). Anthropic calls the guide the authoritative source for exam scope.

## Is there an official practice test?

Anthropic's FAQ says the practice exam on its previous platform was retired in the move to Pearson. Each exam guide includes sample questions. These notes have 10 original practice items per domain, and the [diagnostic page](diagnostic.md) lists free timed diagnostics.

## Where can I find Claude certification exam questions?

Only the sample questions in the official guides come from Anthropic. Be wary of sites selling "real exam questions": those are dumps, which are often wrong and against the exam rules. Practise on original items written to the published domains instead.

## How much does the exam cost?

Prices are set per exam and listed on the [Partner Academy certifications page](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/page/partner-certifications). Anthropic's FAQ says discounts follow your organization's Claude Partner Network tier, and the fee applies to each attempt.

## How do I register?

Sign in to the Anthropic Partner Academy with your partner company email, open your exam on the certifications page, select Register, confirm your exam and profile, then continue to Pearson to choose online proctoring or a test center and book a time.

## Can I take it online?

Yes. Every exam can be taken online with Pearson OnVUE or at a Pearson test center. Run Pearson's system test before exam day, especially on a work device or corporate network.

## Is the exam open book? Can I use AI tools?

No. The exams are closed book. Notes, documentation, translation tools and AI assistants are not allowed.

## What languages is the exam in?

English and Japanese, according to Anthropic's FAQ. If English is your second language, you can request a time extension through Pearson.

## How many times can I retake it?

Up to four attempts per exam in any rolling 12-month period, with waiting periods of 14 days after the first attempt, 30 days after the second and 90 days after the third. See [retakes](scoring.md#retakes).

## How long is the certification valid?

12 months from the date it is awarded. Renewal on time is a free, non-proctored assessment on the Anthropic Partner Academy, according to Anthropic's FAQ.

## Do I need Architect, Foundations before Architect, Professional?

No. They are separate exams with no formal prerequisite. Anthropic says Foundations is the natural place to start, and it does not upgrade to Professional automatically.

## How hard is the exam? How long should I study?

Anthropic does not publish pass rates. In our guidance, someone who already does the work the exam covers can be ready in about a week of focused study using the [7-day plan](study-plans.md#7-day-plan). Someone newer to the role should add hands-on practice in the heaviest domains first.

## What do I get when I pass?

A Credly digital badge you can share, and a PDF certificate you can download from Credly.

## Do these notes reflect the real exam?

They follow the official exam guides' domains, weights and skill statements. The practice items are original and written for these notes, not taken from Anthropic's exams.
